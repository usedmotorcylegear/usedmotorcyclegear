Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information usedmotorcyclegear.com collects, how we use it, and what rights you have over your data. We take your privacy seriously and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google. You may also choose to provide a username, location, and other profile details.

Listing information. When you post a listing, we collect the content you provide — photos, item descriptions, price, condition, and category.

Transaction information. When you make or receive a payment, we record information about the transaction including the amount, item, buyer ID, and seller ID. Payment processing is handled by Stripe — we do not store your credit card number, bank account information, or any raw payment data on our servers.

Messages. Messages sent between buyers and sellers through the platform are stored so both parties can access their conversation history.

Usage data. We may collect standard web analytics data such as pages visited, browser type, device type, and referring URL. This data is collected in aggregate and is not linked to your identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain the platform
  • Process transactions and manage escrow
  • Enable messaging between buyers and sellers
  • Send you transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates, dispute notifications)
  • Detect and prevent fraud and prohibited listings
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Improve the Site

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for behavioral advertising or share it with advertising networks.

3. Information Shared with Other Users

When you post a listing, your username and general location (if provided) are visible to other users. Your email address and phone number are never shown to other users.

When a transaction is completed, the seller receives the buyer's shipping address in order to ship the item. The buyer does not receive the seller's personal contact information beyond what is visible on their public profile.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Site:

  • Supabase — database, user authentication, and file storage. Your account data and listings are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe collects and processes your payment information directly and is subject to its own privacy policy. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • AfterShip — shipment tracking. Tracking numbers and carrier information are shared with AfterShip to monitor delivery status. AfterShip Privacy Policy
  • Google — if you use Google Sign-In, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us as part of authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — web hosting. Vercel may log standard server access data (IP addresses, request logs). Vercel Privacy Policy

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and to maintain your session. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but doing so may prevent you from signing in or using the Site.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and listing data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes (for example, transaction records required for tax or fraud prevention purposes).

Messages may be retained for up to 12 months after a transaction is completed to assist with dispute resolution.

7. Your Rights — California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know — You can request a list of the personal information we have collected about you and how it is used.
  • Right to delete — You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to opt out of sale — We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to non-discrimination — We will not treat you differently for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@usedmotorcyclegear.com. We will respond within 45 days.

8. Your Rights — European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to our processing of your data

Our legal basis for processing your data is performance of a contract (to operate your account and fulfill transactions) and legitimate interests (fraud prevention, platform security).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@usedmotorcyclegear.com.

9. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication through Supabase, and row-level security policies on our database. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at privacy@usedmotorcyclegear.com.
