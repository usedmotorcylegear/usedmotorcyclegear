Buying Used ICON Gear — What to Know

Is used ICON gear worth it?

ICON makes bold urban and sport street gear at accessible prices, with CE armor in its jackets and gloves. Used ICON is a great budget entry — check the armor inserts, stitching, and shell for abrasion, and confirm it was never crashed (every listing here is seller-certified).

How does ICON sizing run?

ICON street and sport fits tend to run true-to-size to slightly slim. Mesh and textile jackets fit a bit more relaxed; leather sport pieces are snugger. Go by the seller's measurements.

What ICON gear can I find used here?

Jackets, gloves, pants, and boots — ICON's Field Armor, Hypersport, and Contra lines are common. We don't sell helmets, so ICON helmets aren't listed.

How much should used ICON gear cost?

ICON is affordable new, so used prices are low — typically 30–50% of retail. It's a great option for first-time riders building a kit.
