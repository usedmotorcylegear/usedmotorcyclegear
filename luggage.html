Buying Used Motorcycle Luggage — What to Know

What types of motorcycle luggage are available?

The main types are tank bags (mount on the fuel tank via magnets or straps, great for maps and quick-access items), saddlebags (hang on either side of the rear wheel for maximum capacity), tail bags (strap to the pillion seat, versatile for day trips), and hard top cases (lockable, waterproof, mount to a rear rack). Soft luggage is lighter; hard luggage is more secure and weather-proof.

Will used motorcycle luggage fit my bike?

Hard luggage systems (panniers, top cases) are usually brand- and rack-specific. Givi, SW-Motech, and Hepco & Becker systems require a matching mounting rack for your specific motorcycle model. Soft luggage is more universal — saddlebags use straps, and tank bags use magnets (not compatible with plastic or carbon tanks) or straps. Always confirm fitment before buying.

What should I check when buying used motorcycle luggage?

For soft bags: inspect zippers (the most common failure point), look for tears or delamination in waterproof coatings, and check all mounting straps and buckles. For hard luggage: test the locking mechanism, inspect mounting brackets for cracks, and confirm hinges open and close smoothly. Scuffs on the outside are cosmetic; compromised locks or broken brackets are deal-breakers.

How much does used motorcycle luggage cost?

Soft saddlebag sets ($80–$200 new) sell used for $30–$80. Kriega and Ortlieb drybag-style bags hold value well and sell used for $40–$120. Givi or SW-Motech hard pannier sets ($300–$600 new) go for $100–$250 used, often without the mounting hardware. Top cases sell separately for $50–$150. Always clarify what's included — rack, locks, and inner bags add value.
