Our standard

We only sell gear that's
never been crashed in.

A jacket that's been laid down looks fine from across the room. The leather is scuffed, the armor has compressed, the seams have stressed — but it'll still zip up and feel normal. It just won't protect you the same way it did before. Same goes for riding pants, boots that went through pavement, or body armor that absorbed an impact.

Every seller on this platform agrees to one rule: list only gear that has never been in an accident. That means gear you outgrew, gear you upgraded from, gear that just sat in the garage. Not gear that saved your life — keep that one for yourself.

How it works
Buying gear
01

Browse and find what you need

Filter by category, condition, and location. Message the seller if you have questions before committing.

02

Buy outright or make an offer

Hit Buy Now to pay the listed price, or submit a lower offer. Sellers can accept, decline, or counter.

03

Pay through Stripe

Your money goes into escrow — not to the seller. They can't touch it until after you've received the item.

04

Get your gear, then funds release

Once delivered, you have 3 days to flag any issues. After that the seller gets paid. No disputes, no drama.

Selling gear
01

Post your listing — it's free

Upload a few photos, describe the item honestly, set your price. Takes 5 minutes and goes live immediately.

02

Talk to buyers on-platform

All messages happen through the site. Your phone number and email stay private the whole time.

03

Ship it and enter your tracking

Once a buyer pays, pack it up and ship it. Enter the tracking number in your Orders page to start the clock.

04

Get paid after delivery

3 days after confirmed delivery, your payout releases. We take 13.33% to cover Stripe fees and buyer protection.

The ground rules
—

No crashed gear, ever. If it's been down, it's done. Don't list it here. We trust sellers to be honest about this — and buyers can flag listings that seem off.

—

Photos have to be real. No stock images, no manufacturer photos. Snap a few shots of the actual item before you list it.

—

Describe it accurately. Scuffs, worn stitching, faded panels — mention it. Buyers appreciate honesty and it keeps disputes off your plate.

—

Keep it on the platform. Taking payments outside the site voids buyer protection for both parties. Not worth it.
