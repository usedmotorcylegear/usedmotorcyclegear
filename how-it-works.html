Buy and sell used riding gear,
the right way.

A marketplace built for riders. Every transaction is protected — no sketchy meetups, no Venmo disputes, no ghosted sellers.

For Buyers
1

Find your gear

Browse listings by category, condition, and location. Every listing includes photos, a full description, and the seller's location.

2

Buy or make an offer

Pay the asking price instantly or submit an offer. If the seller accepts, you'll get a checkout link sent directly to your messages.

3

Pay securely via Stripe

Your payment is held in escrow — the seller doesn't receive a dime until after you've had time to confirm the item arrived as described.

4

Receive your gear

Once the seller ships and enters a tracking number, funds are released 3 days after confirmed delivery — giving you a window to flag any issues.

For Sellers
1

Post your listing for free

Upload photos, write a description, set your price. Takes less than 5 minutes. Your listing goes live immediately.

2

Get buyers through the site

Buyers can message you, make offers, or buy outright. All communication stays on-platform — your contact info stays private.

3

Ship it and enter tracking

Once paid, pack up the gear, ship it, and enter the tracking number in your Orders page. That starts the 3-day delivery clock.

4

Get paid

After the 3-day window closes with no disputes, your payout is released. A 13.33% platform fee covers payment processing and buyer protection.
