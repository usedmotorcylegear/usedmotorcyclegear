Buying a Used Motorcycle Jacket — What You Need to Know

Is it safe to buy a used motorcycle jacket?

Yes — provided it was never worn in a crash. Unlike helmets, motorcycle jackets don't have a single-impact failure rule. CE-rated armor at the shoulders, elbows, and back remains effective as long as the materials are intact and the jacket has never taken impact. Every seller on this site certifies that their gear is crash-free before listing.

What should I look for when buying a used jacket?

Check for: CE armor ratings at shoulders and elbows (Level 2 preferred), intact stitching at all seams — especially cuffs and underarms — working zippers and velcro closures, and no hidden abrasion or scuff damage on the shell or lining. Ask for photos of the back protector pocket and armor inserts.

Leather vs. textile — which is better used?

Leather offers superior abrasion resistance and is the go-to for sport and track riding. Textile jackets (Cordura, ballistic nylon) are more versatile — many are waterproof and breathable, ideal for commuting and touring. Both hold up well used. The right choice depends on your riding style.

How much should I pay for a used motorcycle jacket?

A jacket in good condition typically sells for 35–60% of its original retail price. A jacket that retailed at $400 should cost $140–$240 used. Like-new items command closer to 60% of retail; "fair" condition drops to 30–40%. Compare the listing price against current retail to gauge the deal.
