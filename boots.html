Buying Used Motorcycle Boots — What to Know

Are used motorcycle boots safe to buy?

Yes. Unlike helmets, motorcycle boots don't have a single-impact discard rule. Check the ankle protection inserts, toe cap, and heel counter for any deformation. Make sure the buckles, straps, or zippers still function correctly and the sole hasn't separated or worn through at the heel.

What types of motorcycle boots are there?

Race boots offer the highest protection but are stiff and impractical for walking. Adventure boots balance protection with walkability — great for dual-sport and ADV riders. Touring boots look like normal footwear but have hidden reinforcement. Street/commuter boots are low-profile and comfortable for daily use.

How do I get the right size in used boots?

Most motorcycle boots use European sizing. Compare the EU size to your US size using a standard chart (EU 42 ≈ US 9 men's). If you're between sizes, ask the seller to measure the insole length in centimeters. Leather boots stretch slightly with use; synthetic boots do not.

What brands hold their value best?

Sidi, Alpinestars, TCX, Gaerne, and REV'IT! are consistently well-rated and hold value. Sidi in particular is known for replaceable parts — buckles and soles can be rebuilt, making used Sidis an especially smart buy.
