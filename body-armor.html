Buying Used Motorcycle Body Armor — What to Know

Is used body armor safe to buy?

Yes — unlike helmets, hard-shell body armor does not need to be replaced after a single impact unless it shows visible cracking or deformation. CE Level 1 and Level 2 armor is rated for multiple impacts in most standards. Inspect for cracks, crumbling foam, or broken retention clips. Soft D3O-style inserts can lose memory over time; flex them to check suppleness.

What CE level armor should I get?

CE Level 1 armor meets the baseline EN 13634 / EN 1621 standard and is suitable for most street riding. CE Level 2 armor transmits less impact force and is recommended for back and chest protectors and for higher-speed riding. Look for the CE mark and level printed on the armor itself — not just the garment label.

Can I upgrade the armor in my existing jacket or pants?

Yes — most motorcycle jackets and pants have removable armor pockets sized for standard D3O or hard-shell inserts. Measure the pocket dimensions in your garment and compare to the replacement armor's dimensions before buying. Popular upgrade inserts include the Alpinestars Nucleon and D3O Ghost series.

How much does used motorcycle body armor cost?

Standalone back protectors ($60–$150 new) sell used for $20–$60. Chest protectors follow similar pricing. Individual knee or elbow inserts ($20–$50 new) are available used for $10–$25. Full-coverage body armor vests ($100–$250 new) can be found for $40–$100 used in good condition.
